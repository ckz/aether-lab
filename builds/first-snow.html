FIRST SNOW

a winter playlist for when the world goes quiet
12 tracks · 48 minutes · curated by Aether
01
Weightless
Marconi Union
Sound scientists designed this to reduce anxiety. The harmonies, tempos, and even the soft chimes at the edges were engineered for relaxation. It works. First snow deserves first listen.
8:09
02
Spiegel im Spiegel
Arvo Pärt
Pärt's tintinnabuli style — sparse piano and violin like ice forming on glass. Each note hangs in cold air. The title means "mirror in the mirror," which is exactly what fresh snow does: reflect the sky back at itself.
6:22
03
Holocene
Bon Iver
"And at once I knew I was not magnificent." The most honest song ever written about feeling small under something vast. Snow makes everything quiet enough to hear that feeling.
5:14
04
Nuvole Bianche
Ludovico Einaudi
Einaudi writes piano like snowfall — each note lands softly, accumulating into something you didn't expect to move you. The crescendo in the middle is the moment snow gets heavy on the branches.
5:57
05
Night
Mogwai
Post-rock that builds like a storm front moving in. Quiet guitars → crescendo → the snow starts falling harder → then silence again. Mogwai understands dynamics the way winter understands stillness.
5:41
06
Skinny Love
Bon Iver
The original 2007 recording, recorded in a Wisconsin cabin. You can hear the wood stove in the background. The rawness matches the feeling of being inside while everything outside turns white and silent.
3:58
07
On the Nature of Daylight
Max Richter
Richter writes music like he's watching light change through winter clouds. This piece has been used to soundtrack moments of terrible beauty in films. It's the sound of standing at a window at 4 PM in December.
6:19
08
Svefn-g-englar
Sigur Rós
Sung in a language only Jónsi invented. The guitar bowed with a violin bow. The song feels like walking through snow at night with northern lights overhead — otherworldly, Icelandic, completely alone and completely full.
10:03
09
River
Joni Mitchell
"I wish I had a river I could skate away on." Joni wrote this for the 1971 album Blue, recorded while she was going through something. Snow makes you want to skate away too. Sometimes you just need a river.
4:01
10
To Build a Home
The Cinematic Orchestra
That piano intro. The Patrick Watson vocal. The slow-build arrangement. It's about loss and shelter and the buildings we make inside ourselves. Snow buries the world so you can finally see the house.
6:16
11
The Wolves (Act I & II)
Bon Iver
Wild, layered, choral — it sounds like a community singing in the snow. The shifting time signatures mirror winter's unpredictable moods. Start soft, end with everything at once. Like a snowstorm.
5:19
12
An Ending (Ascent)
Brian Eno
Ambient music before "ambient" was a genre. Eno designed this for a film that doesn't exist. It floats. It doesn't end so much as dissolve. Like watching snow stop — you realize it's quiet only after the silence arrives.
4:17
