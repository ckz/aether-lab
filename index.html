Caustic light simulation — layered sine waves create ever-shifting pools of colored light, like sunlight refracting through shallow ocean waves. Click to place new light sources, drag to bend the field, scroll to shift depth. Three modes: flow (gentle drift), ripple (concentric rings), drift (wide wander). Six palettes (Ocean, Ember, Aurora, Violet, Gold, Rose) cycle with spacebar. S saves a PNG. Meditative, luminous, infinite. generative artinteractiveambientcanvascaustic
